Privacy & Security

Privacy Policy

1. Overview

NexaBull values your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is stored, and the measures we employ to safeguard your personal and cryptographic data.

2. Data Collection

We collect essential operational information including email addresses provided during registration, public wallet addresses used for transaction routing, and technical diagnostics to maintain platform uptime.

3. Private Keys & Non-Custodial Safety

NexaBull does not store or have access to your private cryptographic keys. You are solely responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your seed phrases and wallet credentials.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Information is disclosed only when required by valid legal process or to protect platform infrastructure against malicious attacks.

5. Inquiries

Questions regarding data privacy may be directed to privacy@nexabull.live.
