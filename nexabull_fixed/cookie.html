Cookie Policy

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and web storage technologies are small text files placed on your browser to recognize returning sessions and remember preferences.

2. How We Use Them

NexaBull uses strictly necessary cookies and local storage tokens for session authentication, dark mode preference caching, and security verification.

3. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings. Note that disabling strictly necessary cookies may impair authentication and site navigation.
